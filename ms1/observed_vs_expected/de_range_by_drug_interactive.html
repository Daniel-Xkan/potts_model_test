ΔE double range by drug — Stanford substitutions at IAS-USA positions

One dot per DRM pair of that drug — every Stanford HIVDB substitution at the drug's IAS-USA codon positions, paired up, kept when the bivariate marginal in the MSA is non-zero. The x position is the 95–5 percentile spread of ΔE double over the whole alignment. Raise the ΔΔE threshold and the dots drop out, the boxes recompute, and the rows re-sort on the new medians.

Box = IQR across the drug's surviving pairs, heavy line = median, whiskers = the furthest pair within 1.5×IQR, dots = the pairs themselves (jittered vertically only; the x value is exact). ΔΔE here is evaluated on the consensus sequence, the same convention as the ms0_5 ddE tables — G140S-Q148H returns 8.51. A pair reachable from several drugs is drawn once per drug with the same x. Mutations from ms0_5/*/data/drug_resistant_pair_*_stanford.tsv; drug assignment by IAS-USA 2025 positions.
